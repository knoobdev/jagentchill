# Film story

## From an idea to a story
Take what the author gave (a sentence, a synopsis, a chapter) and state the premise in one line: who wants what, and what stands in the way. If you cannot, the idea is not ready; ask for the missing part.

Expand in passes: premise, then beats, then scenes. Keep the author's own words where they wrote any.

## Episodes
Split where a question is raised, not where a word count is reached. Each episode needs its own turn and ends on something unresolved. State the target length per episode and keep episodes within a fifth of each other.

## Style bible
Write down once what must stay constant:
- Period, place and palette.
- Visual style in one clause, reused verbatim in every prompt.
- Each main character in two lines: look and manner.
- What the film never shows.

## Creative direction
Record the choices that are taste, not fact (pace, humour, how violent, how stylised) so later stages do not re-decide them. Change the bible deliberately; a change stales everything made from it.

Read and update the film with `jc_film_get` and `jc_film_update_story`.
