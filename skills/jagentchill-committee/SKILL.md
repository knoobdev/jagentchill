# Committee

Use a committee for a question with real disagreement and real stakes, where independent views are worth their cost. Three members are usually enough; five is the ceiling.

## Convening
Open a room with `jc_room_create`. Give every member the same question and the same facts, and ask for a position with reasons before anyone sees another's answer. Views formed together are one view repeated.

## Positions
Each member returns: its answer, its two strongest reasons, what would change its mind, and its confidence in plain words.

## Resolving dissent
- If all agree for different reasons, check that the reasons do not contradict each other.
- If they split, state the disagreement as one question about a fact or a value. A fact is settled by checking it. A value goes to the person who owns the decision.
- One round of replies to the opposing position is allowed. After that the convener decides and records the minority view.

Never resolve by counting votes alone, and never drop the dissent from the record.

## Result
Report the decision, the reasons, the dissent and what remains unverified.
