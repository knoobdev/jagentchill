# Advisor

Ask for advice when a choice is costly to reverse and you can state it clearly. You stay the owner: the advisor changes nothing.

## Asking
Give the advisor:
- The decision, as a question with the options you see.
- What you would choose and why.
- The facts it rests on, and which you have not verified.

Do not give your conclusion as the task. Ask "what is wrong with this?", not "confirm this".

## Being the advisor
Answer the question asked. Say which option you would take and the single strongest reason. Name the assumption that, if false, changes your answer. Do not start doing the work, and do not soften a disagreement.

## Using the advice
Weigh it; do not obey it. If you go against it, write down why. One round is normal, two is the limit: more is a sign the question was not clear.

## When not to ask
Do not ask about reversible, cheap choices, or to avoid deciding. Advice costs tokens and time.
