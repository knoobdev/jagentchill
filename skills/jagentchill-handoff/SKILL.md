# Hand-off

## Giving work away
Send a context package, not a pointer to the conversation:
1. **Goal**: the result wanted and how it will be judged.
2. **State**: what exists now, by name.
3. **Decisions already made**, and by whom.
4. **Constraints**: budget, deadline, what is off limits.
5. **Open questions** you could not settle.

After a hand-off the work is theirs. Do not keep editing the same thing.

## Giving work back
Return four sections, in this order:
- **Changes**: what you did, precisely, and where.
- **Evidence**: what you ran or looked at that shows it works, with the result.
- **Uncertainties**: what you did not verify, what you assumed.
- **Counter-arguments**: the strongest case that your result is wrong or that another approach is better.

An empty section is written as "none", never left out.

## Honesty
Report a partial result as partial. A hand-back that hides a failure costs the receiver more than the failure itself.
